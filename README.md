# em·ji·ve — website

The website of em·ji·ve, a jewelry brand whose pieces are designed in 3D and printed in metal (steel, silver or bronze), each one made to order.

The pieces come in **series**, collections with a theme of their own. The first, *Bones*, is a set of rings drawn from the shapes of the human skeleton. The homepage belongs to the current series: it opens on that series' own animation (an x-ray of a hand wearing the rings), then pulls back to a **wheel** of every piece in 3D, which the visitor turns to bring one to the front. A plain **list** of the pieces is one tap away for a quicker look.

Each piece has its own page:

- **Photographs** and a description.
- **The piece in 3D**, to turn freely, in the chosen metal (with its composition and how it is printed) and, for some pieces, one of several designs.
- **Its specifications**: the size (set on a dial under the piece, with a guide to find one's own), a front and a side view (a tap turns the piece to one and lays its outline over it, its length, width and depth marked), and what it costs to make.

Visitors build a **selection** as they go, which follows them from page to page. They can review it on an order page, change or remove an item, and accept the terms.

## Where it stands

The site is a working prototype whose look keeps evolving. Most pieces are still waiting for their photographs, descriptions and prices. The order page ends with a "Proceed to checkout" button, but there is no payment step behind it yet. The archives, creation process and terms pages are placeholders.

## Working on it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open the address it prints. Everything else, from how the site is built to how a new piece is added, is in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) and [`dev-guidelines/`](dev-guidelines/).
